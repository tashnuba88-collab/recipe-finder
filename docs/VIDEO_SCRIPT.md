# Demo video outline (about 3 minutes)

Record the screen and talk over it. Classmates will leave feedback, so say clearly what you built and what you'd like them to look at.

1. **Intro, 15 seconds.** Name, project name, one sentence on what it does and who it's for.
2. **Search, 40 seconds.** Type a recipe name, then change the category. Point out that the text stays in the URL and that the back button returns to it.
3. **Recipe page, 30 seconds.** Open a recipe. Show ingredients, method and the country card.
4. **Second API, 30 seconds.** Say that recipes come from TheMealDB and country facts come from REST Countries. Click the cuisine link and show the cuisine page.
5. **Favorites, 25 seconds.** Save two recipes, open the Saved page, reload the browser to show they persist.
6. **Errors, 15 seconds.** Search for nonsense to show the empty state. Optionally turn off your wifi to show the error message.
7. **Code, 30 seconds.** Show `useAsync.js`, the Favorites context and `AREA_TO_CODE`. Explain what each does in a sentence.
8. **Challenges and next steps, 20 seconds.** Name one real problem you hit and how you solved it. Mention that Part 2 will add a Python API and database.
9. **Ask for feedback, 10 seconds.** Name one thing you want reviewers to comment on.
